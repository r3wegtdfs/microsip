MicroSIP online translator

You can help in translating MicroSIP. No registration needed. Find your language and click "translate".

If you are confident that you can translate microsip better than what is currently available, simply select the appropriate language and follow the instructions.


All entered data is post-moderated, so don’t worry that your translation will be spoiled by someone. However, we recommend that you save a copy of the translation locally.

In case of a possible conflict between the authors of translations, do not hesitate to contact us for help via the contact form.


We are grateful to all the users who contribute to MicroSIP and make it available to different people from all over the world.
