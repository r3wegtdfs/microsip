Support MicroSIP Development

MicroSIP is actively maintained and used in real-world VoIP environments. Your support helps keep MicroSIP reliable, compatible with modern systems, and actively developed.

This contribution is voluntary and non-refundable. No goods or services are provided in return.

How your support helps

Developing and maintaining a reliable SIP softphone requires continuous work long after the initial release.

MicroSIP is used in office systems, remote work environments, and VoIP infrastructures where reliability is essential.

Ongoing work includes:

  • Maintaining compatibility with SIP standards and providers
  • Fixing bugs and improving stability
  • Adapting to Windows updates and system changes
  • Updating and maintaining third-party components
  • Improving usability and user experience
  • Testing across different environments and deployment scenarios
